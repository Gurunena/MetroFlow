Our story

Booking a bus seat should take a minute, not a morning

Reserving a long-distance seat in Sri Lanka has long meant ringing a depot that may not answer, asking a friend near the stand to hold a place, or simply turning up early and hoping. Timetables live on noticeboards, fares are quoted from memory, and the only record of a reservation is a name in a notebook. Passengers cannot see what is available, and operators cannot see what they have already sold.

The timetable already exists. It was just never anywhere you could reach it.

MetroFlow puts that whole process online. Every service, seat map and fare sits in one database, so searching a route, picking a specific seat, paying and managing the trip afterwards all happen in the same place. Operators get a dashboard instead of a notebook, passengers get a reference number instead of a promise, and the seat you chose is still yours when the bus pulls in.

What drives us

Mission & vision

Our mission

To replace manual bus reservations with one dependable online platform — so any passenger with a phone can find a service, choose a seat and confirm a booking in under a minute, and every operator can see their occupancy in real time.

Our vision

A single connected view of public road transport across Sri Lanka, where routes, timetables and seat availability are open and accurate — and planning a trip from Jaffna to Matara is as ordinary as checking the weather.

What you can do here

Search buses

Pick your route and date and see every matching service with its departure time, journey length, fare and seats remaining. Filter by time of day or price and sort by whichever matters most.

Find buses

Choose your seat

An interactive seat map of the actual coach: taken seats are locked, free ones are yours to click. Window, aisle or front row — you decide, rather than finding out when you board.

Book in minutes

Name, phone, seats, done. Your booking gets a reference number straight away so there is always something concrete to quote at the counter or on the phone.

Manage and cancel trips

Every booking lives under My Trips. Look up a reference, check the details before you travel, or cancel a seat you no longer need and free it for someone else.

Under the bonnet

Built with

MetroFlow is a full-stack web application: semantic HTML and Bootstrap on the outside, a jQuery Ajax layer in between, and an Express REST API over MongoDB doing the real work. Nothing is stored in the browser — every bus, seat, booking and message lives in the database.

  • HTML5Semantic page structure
  • Bootstrap 5Responsive grid & components
  • jQueryDOM, events and templating
  • AjaxPages update without reloading
  • JSONThe format every request speaks
  • Node.jsThe server runtime
  • Express REST APIRouting and validation
  • MongoDBBuses, bookings and users
