Project at a glance

System modules

The nine modules the assignment asks for.

  • 1 Bus Management — add, edit and retire buses, with seat totals and fares.
  • 2 Route Management — the origin and destination pairs the network serves.
  • 3 Schedule Management — departure and arrival times per travel date.
  • 4 Bus Search — find services by route, date and passenger count, with filters and sorting.
  • 5 Seat Management — the per-coach seat map and which seats are already sold.
  • 6 Passenger Management — accounts, sign in and the passenger's own details.
  • 7 Booking Management — create, confirm and cancel a booking against real seat stock.
  • 8 Booking Search — look a booking up by reference and review it before travelling.
  • 9 REST API — the JSON endpoints under /api that every page talks to.
